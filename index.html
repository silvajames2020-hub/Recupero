From data access to recovered cash - in as little as one week.

Audit & Discovery

We review your deduction history, identify the largest recovery opportunities, and map the retailers causing the most invalid claims. Typically complete within the first week.

We don't stop at the first pass. We monitor new deductions as they come in, maintain your dispute pipeline, and provide monthly reporting on recovered funds and deduction trends.
